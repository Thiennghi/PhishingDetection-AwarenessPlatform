    Security Awareness Guide

    Learn how to identify social engineering red flags before interacting with malicious links.

    Phishing Red Flags

    • Sense of artificial urgency (e.g., "Account suspended within 24 hours").
    • Generic greetings ("Dear Customer" instead of your name).
    • Misspelled domain names in sender addresses or links.

    Common Financial Scams

    • Unexpected prize, inheritance, or job offers requiring upfront fees.
    • Requests for non-standard payment methods (crypto, gift cards, wire transfer).
    • Impersonation of government officials or tech support agents.
